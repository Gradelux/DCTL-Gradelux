## 07 · Philosophy — `Gradelux_FB_07_philosophy.png`

Color first. Contrast later.

Many "film looks" get their punch from heavy contrast. ColorLab builds color instead: film
density, subtractive saturation and hue-by-hue separation, with skin tone protection built in.

The result is rich and dimensional without crushing blacks. Contrast and print character get
their own tool, PrintLab.

## Tips

- **Carousel:** post 02 + 03 + 04 + 05 + 06 together as one carousel ad. It shows the range of looks.
- **Best follow-up:** once ColorLab is tested, real before/after frames from your own footage will
  beat any graphic. Use the same layout and swap the palette art for a split-screen frame.
- **Editing the images:** change text or colors in `src/ads.html`, then run
  `node marketing/facebook/src/render.cjs` from the repo root to re-export all PNGs.
